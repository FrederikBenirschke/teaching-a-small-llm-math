Step 2. Find the area of both of those walls: 6 x 2 = 12 square meters.
Step 3. Find the area of the third wall: 5 x 2 = 10 square meters.
Step 4. Find the area of the final wall: 4 x 2 = 8 square meters.
Step 5. Add all the areas: 12 + 10 + 8 = 30 square meters.
Step 6. Find the cans, because each can covers 2 square meters: 30 / 2 = 15 cans.
Check: 15 cans cover 15 x 2 = 30 square meters, which equals the total wall area.
Answer: 15
