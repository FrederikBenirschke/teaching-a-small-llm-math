Step 1. Note that the pack is cheaper per balloon, because 10 single balloons would cost 10 x 0.5 = 5 dollars while the pack costs 3 dollars, so he should buy one pack.
Step 2. Find how many balloons are still needed after the pack: 14 - 10 = 4 balloons.
Step 3. Find the cost of those single balloons: 4 x 0.5 = 2 dollars.
Step 4. Add the pack price: 3 + 2 = 5 dollars.
Check: Buying all 14 singly would cost 14 x 0.5 = 7 dollars, so 5 dollars is cheaper, and two packs would cost 6 dollars, also more.
Answer: 5

We need how many pounds Michael must lose in May to reach his goal.
Step 1. Find the weight he has lost so far, in March and April: 3 + 4 = 7 pounds.
Step 2. Subtract from his goal of 10 pounds: 10 - 7 = 3 pounds.
Check: 3 + 4 + 3 = 10 pounds over March, April and May, which meets the goal.
Answer: 3

### valid-99
We need how many more pages Cyrus must write to finish the 500-page book.
Step 1. Find the pages on the second day, because he wrote twice the first day's 25: 2 x 25 = 50 pages.
Step 2. Find the pages on the third day, because he wrote twice the second day's 50: 2 x 50 = 100 pages.
Step 3. Find the total written in four days, including the 10 pages on the fourth day: 25 + 50 + 100 + 10 = 185 pages.
Step 4. Subtract from 500 pages: 500 - 185 = 315 pages.
Check: 185 written plus 315 remaining is 185 + 315 = 500 pages, the full book.
Answer: 315
